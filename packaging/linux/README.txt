Installing JARVIS on Linux
==========================

1. Open a terminal in this folder (right-click inside the folder > "Open in Terminal").
2. Type:   ./install.sh     and press Enter.
3. Open JARVIS from your applications menu (or type: jarvis).
   A short setup guide helps you choose its AI, test your microphone and pick a voice.

No administrator password is needed. Your data is kept in ~/.local/share/jarvis.

To remove JARVIS later:   ~/.local/lib/jarvis/uninstall.sh
(It asks before deleting your data.)
